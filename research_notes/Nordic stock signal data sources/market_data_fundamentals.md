## Q1. Free/unofficial sources: which work in 2026, and how (exact endpoints)?

### Takeaway
In autumn 2026 three free EOD routes for Nordic shares still work: Yahoo via yfinance (NO/SE/DK/FI, around 10 years of daily history plus basic fundamentals, but rate-limited and "personal use only"), Nasdaq's undocumented `api.nasdaq.com/api/nordic/...` JSON API (SE/DK/FI/IS only, with no Oslo), and Euronext's undocumented `getFullDownloadAjax` CSV (Oslo, now limited to about 2 years per request). Since early 2026 Stooq requires a captcha-issued API key, Investing.com scraping (investpy) has been broken since 2022, and Ødegaard's site has only Norwegian factor and market series, not stock-level prices.

### Cited Findings

#### Yahoo Finance via yfinance (owner: open-source project; data: Yahoo) [S][L]
- **Status 2026:** The latest release is yfinance **1.7.0 (2026-08-26)**. The 1.x line started with 1.1.0 (2026-01-24), and releases have been frequent (1.2.0 Feb 16, 1.3.0 Apr 16, 1.4.0/1.4.1 May 23/28, 1.5.x Jun–Jul, 1.6.0 Aug 13). The last 0.2.x release was 0.2.66 (2025-09-17) — [PyPI yfinance](https://pypi.org/project/yfinance/)
- **ToS:** The package disclaimer reads "Remember - the Yahoo! finance API is intended for personal use only." Users are told to refer to Yahoo's terms of use for their rights to the data — [PyPI yfinance](https://pypi.org/project/yfinance/)
- **Nordic coverage, third-party verified 2026-08-28:** yfinance 1.4.1 was tested on 10 Nordic tickers (SE main market, First North, NO, DK, FI). It returned 10-year daily OHLCV "without gaps" (e.g., MYCR 2,515 rows 2016–2026; CX 1,110 rows from its 2022 IPO; HARVIA 2,117 rows from its 2018 IPO). Quarterly fundamentals were "fresh" (Q2-2026 for 3 of 4 tested), along with earnings dates, dividends and splits, at 0.2–0.5 s per ticker for a 6-month fetch — [marketscan RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md)
- **Field quirks found in that audit:** `earningsDate` is always null (use `earningsTimestamp`), and `analystCount` is null (use `numberOfAnalystOpinions`, present on all 10). Banks lack debtToEquity/FCF, loss-makers lack PE/dividend fields, and price history carries no currency identifier. RILBA.CO had an encoding artifact in shortName — [marketscan RAPPORT](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md); [marketscan datastack-verifierad-2026-08-28](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/datastack-verifierad-2026-08-28.md)
- **Throughput:** The recommended "batch-12" price download took about 100 s per 300 tickers — [marketscan datastack-verifierad](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/datastack-verifierad-2026-08-28.md)
- **Underlying endpoint (seen in code/logs):** The chart endpoint is `https://query2.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/{SYMBOL}` with params `range`, `interval`, `includePrePost`, `events=div,splits,capitalGains` (from a yfinance debug log) — [yfinance issue #2377](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2377). Other clients call the same path on query1: `https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart` — [MrTrigger/ai-trader lib.rs](https://github.com/MrTrigger/ai-trader/blob/620d554320f1b580c9ba20f955450dc08091d270/service/crates/equity-data/src/lib.rs). Suffixes: `.OL` (all three Oslo lists: Oslo Børs, Euronext Expand, Euronext Growth), `.ST`, `.CO`, `.HE` — [viljarem/Minervinis konfig.py](https://github.com/viljarem/Minervinis/blob/767ceb93249dddfc65b633218c15061fd199ade0/motor/konfig.py); [amauryleproux/olyos-capital](https://github.com/amauryleproux/olyos-capital/blob/df2b03be75f5ccfc4f0afb29ffa7846f4a7a366c/olyos/services/higgons_agent_service.py)
- **Rate limiting (2025–2026):** Users have reported `YFRateLimitError` since 0.2.57 (April 2025) — [yfinance #2422](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2422); [yfinance #2480](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2480). In the maintainer discussion, "Yahoo tends to block IPs for a while" (May 2025). One user throttled to about 100 requests and then a 30 s pause. Yahoo blocks some VPNs. On 2026-01-21 a user was blocked "on the first request", and the maintainer recommended the `yfinance-cache` add-on ("Stop spamming Yahoo, fetch smarter") — [yfinance discussion #2431](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/discussions/2431). Downstream projects still reported rate-limit errors in 2026, per a search summary — [HKUDS/Vibe-Trading #1511](https://github.com/HKUDS/Vibe-Trading/issues/1511); [TradingAgents #437](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents/issues/437)
- **Known adjustment bug class:** Yahoo's dividend adjustment of weekly and monthly bars has been wrong in some cases (closed issue) — [yfinance #1273](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/1273)
- **Environment note:** The coordinator reports that query2.finance.yahoo.com, fc.yahoo.com and finance.yahoo.com remain blocked in this sandbox. The debug log above shows yfinance's history calls going to **query2**, so default yfinance would need those hosts allowed wherever the app runs (inference from [yfinance #2377](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2377)).

### Inferences
- For the MVP, yfinance is the only single free source covering all four countries plus Euronext Growth Oslo and First North. It needs a local cache, throttling (about 100 requests then a pause, or yfinance-cache), and a second source for validation.
- Nasdaq's API is the best free cross-check for SE/DK/FI. The universe endpoint gives ISIN and orderbook ID with authoritative segment membership. Euronext's CSV is the matching cross-check for Oslo, with at most 2 years per call, so chunk the backfill. Because both are undocumented, wrap them behind adapters with health checks.
- The "OSL 400" in the marketscan report most likely means an **HTTP 400 for market=OSL**, not 400 rows. This is consistent with the same repo's explicit statement that OSL is not a Nasdaq market and with Oslo being a Euronext venue.
- Ødegaard's data is useful for the long-term sleeve's factor benchmarks (Norwegian SMB/HML/UMD, market and risk-free returns), but it is no substitute for stock-level prices.

## Q2. Paid APIs with Nordic coverage: venue coverage, delisted coverage and 2026 price

### Takeaway
Börsdata Pro+ (599 SEK or €59 per month; API Pro+-only since 2025-02-01) is the best-value Nordic-specialist API for fundamentals, KPIs, report dates, insider/short/buyback data and 20 years of EOD, but it is licensed for personal use. EODHD (All World EOD from $19.99/mo; Fundamentals $59.99; All-In-One $99.99) is the cheapest global API with OL/ST/CO/HE codes and a delisted-symbol list. It reportedly lacks First North, Spotlight and NGM. FMP needs its top tier for non-US coverage, Twelve Data lists XOSL/XSTO as licence-required or unsupported, and Alpha Vantage and Tiingo are effectively US-centric. 2026 prices for the institutional vendors (LSEG, FactSet, Capital IQ, Bloomberg, Morningstar, Infront, Modular Finance) could not be verified.

## Q3. Point-in-time (as-reported) fundamentals for Nordic value investing

### Takeaway
No free source provides real point-in-time Nordic fundamentals. Among affordable sources, Börsdata is the closest: each report row carries `report_Date` (publication date) plus period start/end, the report currency and a currency ratio. It is limited to the last 20 annual and 40 quarterly/R12 reports, and whether values are as-first-reported or restated is unknown. The practical answer for a personal app is to build your own point-in-time store by snapshotting fundamentals daily and timestamping them with the actual release time (MFN for Sweden; Oslo NewsWeb or other sources for Norway).

### Cited Findings
- Börsdata report rows include `report_Date`, `report_Start_Date`, `report_End_Date`, `currency`, `currency_Ratio` and `broken_Fiscal_Year` alongside the line items — [carlwestman/PyFinModel borsdata_collector.py](https://github.com/carlwestman/PyFinModel/blob/aee035bdb0263105b230eff2f6fda04c937ef1d7/PyFinModeler/data/borsdata_collector.py); [Modern-Borsdata-Client models.py](https://github.com/alexwox/Modern-Borsdata-Client/blob/df4231e4ad72a803202dfd32dfa459c4d03afa49/src/borsdata_client/models.py); `report_End_Date` is also in the official C# client models — [Borsdata-Sweden/API-CSharp-Client ReportQuarterV1.cs](https://github.com/Borsdata-Sweden/API-CSharp-Client/blob/414deeba15f492dc4cdd7a1f61448dfecac3367c/Borsdata.Api.Dal/Model/ReportQuarterV1.cs)

### Inferences
- For backtests, use Börsdata `report_Date` as the availability timestamp (add a 1-day lag to be safe). Treat values as possibly restated until proven otherwise, so backtest results are an upper bound.
- Börsdata's 20/40-report caps give at most about 10 years of quarterly history and 20 years of annual history per company. That is enough for value-factor research but shallow for regime testing.
- For USD-reporting Oslo companies (common among shipping and oil-service names), always compute multiples in a single currency: price × shares in listing currency vs fundamentals × FX at the report date. Börsdata's `currency`/`currency_Ratio` and `&original=1` let you do this explicitly.
- Since every prediction is logged, the app should also log the input snapshot (fundamentals as seen that day). This creates a personal point-in-time dataset over time, independent of any vendor's restatements.

### Gaps
- Whether Börsdata values are as-first-reported or restated, and whether `report_Date` is the actual publication date or a scheduled one, is unverified.
- No information was gathered on institutional point-in-time datasets (S&P Compustat Global point-in-time, LSEG Worldscope, FactSet Fundamentals) for Nordic coverage and pricing.
- Whether EODHD or FMP fundamentals include `filing_date`/`acceptedDate` for Nordic issuers is unverified.
- Norway lacks a free, verified dated-release feed (NewsWeb needs a headless browser or the `obns-api.euronext.cloud` host). Denmark and Finland were not investigated.

## Q4. Analyst data (consensus, revisions, recommendations, target prices): free vs paid

### Takeaway
Free options are thin. MarketScreener's free consensus page (mean/median/high/low target price, buy/hold/sell counts, rating, forward multiples) can be scraped politely, and Yahoo exposes `numberOfAnalystOpinions` for Nordic tickers. No free API offers estimate-revision history, so the app must snapshot daily to build its own revision series. A 2026 audit concluded there is "no free source" for Nordic small-cap consensus. Paid consensus (Modular Finance, Infront/Vara, LSEG I/B/E/S, FactSet) could not be verified for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- MarketScreener (free, HTML only, no API): `https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/{NAME}-{ID}/consensus/` shows target price (mean/median/high/low), buy/hold/sell counts and rating summary. The `/ratings/` page has trader/investor ratings with an "EPS revisions" component. `/calendar/` gives the next earnings date, ex-dividend date, payment date, AGM and split date. `/company-insider-trading/` and `/valuation/` are also free. The ID is found via `https://www.marketscreener.com/search/?q={ticker}`. Anti-bot: 403/429/503 responses, a suggested `MIN_DELAY = 1.5` s, and "Cloudflare protection likely active". "No official scraping ToS published; exercise caution with commercial use" — [gauss314/skills ENDPOINTS.md](https://github.com/gauss314/skills/blob/5156f818420dc9098ac1071e26d34bb7294efe12/skills/marketscreener/references/ENDPOINTS.md)
- A backtester's MarketScreener scraper reads the forward multiples table (P/E, PBR, EV/Sales for "2026 *", "2027 *") and the consensus card ("Number of Analysts, Average target price, Mean consensus rating") — [Tahadaz/backtester_final marketscreener.py](https://github.com/Tahadaz/backtester_final/blob/ff05b35ceae669c8786fe6f37809919dce3c00ea/core/quant_core/fundamentals/consensus/marketscreener.py)
- Yahoo via yfinance: `numberOfAnalystOpinions` was present for all 10 Nordic tickers tested on 2026-08-28 — [marketscan RAPPORT](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md)
- The 2026-08-28 audit lists "Analyst Estimates/Consensus for Nordic small caps: no free source available" as a known gap, and found that Finnhub's free tier has no Nordic small-cap coverage — [marketscan RAPPORT](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md); [datastack-verifierad](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/datastack-verifierad-2026-08-28.md)
- Börsdata's API endpoint list (swagger) has instruments, prices, reports, KPIs, calendars and holdings, but **no estimates/consensus endpoint** — [borsdata-mcp swagger.json](https://github.com/emmanuelay/borsdata-mcp/blob/54efe9d021a2fe23168a2e4db2e571fdd95e4a8f/client/assets/swagger.json)
- Aggregators can disagree on targets. In one 2026-09 note, Stockopedia, MarketScreener, TipRanks and Alpha Spread gave different average targets for the same stock — [ghacupha price_consensus_research.json](https://github.com/ghacupha/unilever_hyperinflation/blob/e749baa58cda6ba77b3b0765ce98f9b874c955ec/examples/2026-09-28_204838/report_workdir/price_consensus_research.json) (non-Nordic example of cross-source divergence)

### Inferences
- MVP: scrape MarketScreener consensus once a day per stock (around 1.5 s spacing, so roughly 600 stocks in 15 minutes) and store snapshots. Compute your own "target-price revision" and "rating drift" signals from the stored history. ## Q6. Exchange data licensing for EOD/delayed data (personal vs non-display; storage and redistribution)

### Takeaway
Both exchange groups treat delayed or EOD data as essentially free for internal or non-display use. Euronext's EMDA says no licence is needed for non-display use of delayed information and that delayed data is free for internal purposes. Nasdaq says delayed Nordic data is "in general free" with no non-display fees for delayed use, and EOD information is usable after the close. Fees start when data is redistributed or sold. The bigger legal risk for a personal app is website and API terms (Yahoo "personal use only", undocumented Nasdaq/Euronext endpoints, Börsdata's no-external-display clause), not exchange fee policies.

### Cited Findings
- Euronext Market Data Agreement (EMDA, effective 1 January 2025): "Contracting parties are not required to obtain a license for their Non-Display Use of Delayed Information", and "the use of delayed market data is free of charge if it is used for internal purposes only". Non-Professional Subscribers are natural persons meeting specific criteria. Redistribution to clients requires a licence via the Order Form and Redistribution Licence Fees — [Euronext EMDA Jan 2025](https://connect2.euronext.com/sites/default/files/documentation/data/EMDA%20General%20Terms%20and%20Conditions%20and%20Policies%20(effective%20January%202025)_0.pdf) (search snippet; PDF blocked). ### Inferences
- A single-user app that stores EOD data and computes signals only for its owner fits the exchanges' internal/non-display, delayed/EOD use. It should not publish prices, charts or derived rankings to others, which would be redistribution.

## Q7. Practical pitfalls for Nordic data (mapping, share classes, currencies, survivorship, adjustments, equity issues)

### Takeaway
The main traps are: (1) identifier fragmentation (Yahoo suffixes, Nasdaq orderbook IDs, Euronext ISIN-MIC, Börsdata insId, three Oslo MICs all mapping to `.OL`); (2) A/B share classes and share counts, which are often off by about 10× in yfinance; (3) reporting currency differing from listing currency (USD/EUR reporters in Oslo); (4) survivorship, since free sources mostly list only live tickers; and (5) adjustments, where Yahoo's dividend logic has bugs and rights issues, frequent on Oslo Børs, have no verified free adjustment feed. Undocumented endpoints also change: Nasdaq migrated, Euronext limited windows to 2 years, and Stooq added keys.

### Cited Findings
- **Share classes:** yfinance can return an A-equivalent share count while the price is for the B share, and for Nordic tickers the balance-sheet share count "regularly deviates ~10×" from `info.sharesOutstanding`. The workaround picks `info` when the ratio is above 2. Mismatched counts produced a "misleading STRONG BUY" — [Nolles15/stockscreen data_fetcher.py](https://github.com/Nolles15/stockscreen/blob/f0b0c43fc7e68f9bc3155536ca142ea9cfdfe73d/engine/data_fetcher.py)
- **Currency:** when `financialCurrency != currency`, per-share fundamentals must be FX-converted, and FX tickers (`{FROM}{TO}=X`) are themselves rate-limited in yfinance — [Nolles15/stockscreen data_fetcher.py](https://github.com/Nolles15/stockscreen/blob/f0b0c43fc7e68f9bc3155536ca142ea9cfdfe73d/engine/data_fetcher.py). yfinance price history "lacks currency identifier" — [marketscan RAPPORT](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md). - **Mapping:** Oslo's XOSL, XOAS and MERK all use `.OL` on Yahoo — [viljarem/Minervinis konfig.py](https://github.com/viljarem/Minervinis/blob/767ceb93249dddfc65b633218c15061fd199ade0/motor/konfig.py). ### Inferences
- Store **unadjusted** OHLCV plus a corporate-actions table (dividends, splits, rights issues and bonus issues with factors), and compute adjusted series yourself. Cross-check monthly against Yahoo's `Adj Close` and Euronext's `adjusted=Y` and flag discrepancies above about 1%. This matters most for Oslo, where rights issues and repair issues are common (background, not quantified here).
- For A/B shares, choose the more liquid class for trading signals (typically B in Sweden) and compute market cap as the sum over classes. Never mix one class's price with another's share count.
- For value metrics on USD- or EUR-reporting Oslo companies, convert fundamentals at the report-date FX rate. For trailing metrics, use average FX for flows and period-end FX for stocks. Log which FX rate was used.
- Survivorship: from day one, persist every ticker ever seen in the universe snapshots, even after delisting, so the app's own logged predictions are survivorship-free. Older backtests need a delisted-capable source (EODHD, or exchange history files).
- Plan for undocumented endpoints to break about yearly. Wrap each source in an adapter with a smoke test and a fallback source.

### Gaps
- No quantified frequency of Oslo rights issues or repair offerings (and their effect on adjusted prices) was gathered.
- Whether Yahoo, Börsdata or Nasdaq adjust for rights issues, and how, is unverified.
- Dual listings (e.g., Oslo plus Stockholm or US ADRs) and their handling across sources were not specifically researched.
