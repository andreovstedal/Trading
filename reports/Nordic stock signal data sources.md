# Nordic signals reward patience, not speed

A personal Nordnet allocation app can collect nearly everything it needs for free from primary disclosures: Oslo Børs NewsWeb's undocumented JSON API, Finansinspektionen's insider CSV and short-position files, Finanstilsynet's short-sale API, MFN's announcement feed, Nordnet's own website JSON and Yahoo's chart API. **Börsdata Pro+ (599 SEK a month)** is the one paid add-on worth buying, for point-in-time fundamentals and structured insider, short and buyback data. The six hosts the user named are only front doors: the data itself sits on at least seven more (`api3.oslo.oslobors.no`, `marknadssok.fi.se`, `ssr.finanstilsynet.no`, `feed.mfn.se`, `storage.mfn.se`, `query2.finance.yahoo.com`, `fc.yahoo.com`), the full allow-list runs to 73 hosts, the core endpoints were verified live only after the research closed (see the addendum at the end), and several registers keep no history, so snapshotting must start on day one. The evidence puts nearly all capital in the long-term sleeve, driven by volatility-scaled 12-1 momentum, quality-filtered earnings-yield value and a low-idiosyncratic-volatility screen, with insider buying, buybacks and disclosed short positions as overlays. The short-term sleeve should begin as a paper ledger, because a Nordnet round trip costs about **0.5–0.75% in liquid names and about 2% in small caps**, more than most documented Nordic short-horizon edges, such as the **0.25% five-day drift** after Finnish insider purchases ([Aalto](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/handle/123456789/499)). The user's hypothesis survives only in a narrower form: anomalies outside the US have not decayed after publication ([Jacobs & Müller](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.06.004)), but the "less advanced traders" in Nordic data are households who lose, with Swedish online investors trailing the market by about **8.5% a year**, mostly through trading costs ([Anderson](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228265693_Trading_and_Under-Diversification)). The exploitable edge is slow information diffusion in neglected names, harvested with patience. An append-only, bitemporal log of every input, prediction, recommendation and fill is what lets the formula improve, and it must run for years: confirming an annualised Sharpe ratio of 1.0 takes about **2.7 years** of daily data, and one of 0.5 about **11 years** ([Bailey & López de Prado](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1821643)).

## Thirteen input channels feed two sleeves very unequally

The app needs thirteen kinds of input, but their value is lopsided. Prices and point-in-time fundamentals feed the factor signals with the strongest Nordic evidence, so they anchor the long sleeve. Regulatory announcements, insider trades, short positions and buybacks are event channels that matter for timing and for vetoes. Retail, analyst, macro and commodity data serve mostly as context, risk filters and hypotheses for the app's own log to test. The table sets out what to collect, where from, and the role each channel earns from the evidence reviewed later.

| Channel | What to collect | Primary source (paid upgrade) | Short-term sleeve | Long-term sleeve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company announcements | Every regulated release: category, timestamp, text, attachments, corrections | NewsWeb API (NO); MFN (SE and all Nordic); Nasdaq company news (DK, FI) | Core: triggers and vetoes | Event history, report dates |
| Insider (PDMR) trades | Open-market buys and sells, role, value, publication time | FI insider CSV (SE); NewsWeb category 1102 (NO); Nasdaq "Managers' Transactions" (DK, FI); Börsdata Pro+ | Confirmation only | Overlay over 1–6 months |
| Short positions | Aggregate %, named holders at ≥0.5%, daily changes | Finanstilsynet SSR API (NO); FI register files (SE) | Avoid or exit after new shorts | Penalty for the most shorted |
| Major shareholdings (flagging) | Holder, threshold, direction | NewsWeb category 1006 (NO); FI notices via MFN (SE) | Log and test | Ownership context |
| Buybacks | Programme terms, executions, share-count changes | NewsWeb 1007 and 1008; MFN releases; Börsdata Pro+ | Announcement event | Overlay (net payout) |

Two structural facts shape the build. First, **Sweden publishes far richer structured data than Norway**. FI's insider register exports 22 typed columns per trade back to July 2016 ([Insynshandel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ErikMalmgren/Insynshandel/main/src/insynshandel/sources/fi.py)), while Norwegian insider trades exist only as free-text or PDF notices in NewsWeb category 1102. Regex parsers mine those so poorly that one tracker found just **146 trades in 90 days** and silently dropped option, incentive-plan and PDF-only notices ([innsider data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remebjornatle/innsider/main/docs/data.json); [innsider notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remebjornatle/innsider/main/CLAUDE.md)). Sweden's aggregate short file sums every position from 0.1% ([FI](https://www.fi.se/sv/publicerat/nyheter/2022/start-for-fortlopande-publicering-av-aggregerade-blankningspositioner/)), Norway publishes only positions of 0.5% and above ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/en/supervision/market-conduct/short-selling-and-reporting-of-short-positions/)), and only Sweden publishes per-fund holdings. Every coefficient in the formula should therefore be country-specific. Second, **several registers cannot be backfilled**. Finanstilsynet's short API returns only positions active in the past two years ([open-apis catalog](https://github.com/trymhaak/open-apis/blob/main/catalog/by-provider/finanstilsynet/shortsalregister.yaml)), FI's aggregate file holds only each issuer's latest figure, and Nordnet exposes owner counts only as a current snapshot ([stockpicker](https://github.com/ryddmo/stockpicker)). Daily snapshots of these sources, started now, become the app's most valuable proprietary dataset.

The rules underneath the data are also moving. Sweden raised the insider-reporting threshold from EUR 5,000 to **EUR 20,000 a year on 4 December 2024**, a structural break in trade counts ([FI Q&A](https://www.fi.se/contentassets/ee10c244dd51477ab830b5f235274acc/2024-12-04-fragor-och-svar-insynshandel.pdf)); Norway's adoption of the same threshold could not be confirmed ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/nyhetsarkiv/nyheter/2023/endringer-i-markedsmisbruksforordningen-og-prospektforordningen)). Finanstilsynet took over disclosure and buyback supervision from Oslo Børs on 1 April 2025 ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/nyhetsarkiv/nyheter/2025/finanstilsynet-har-overtatt-tilsynet-med-lopende-informasjonsplikt-utsatt-offentliggjoring-av/)), and a consultation that closed on 14 September 2026 proposes moving publication of flagging notices from Oslo Børs to Finanstilsynet ([Finanstilsynet hearing](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/nyhetsarkiv/horinger/2026/horing-overforing-av-oppgaven-med-mottak-og-offentliggjoring-av-flaggemeldinger-fra-oslo-bors-til)). Collectors must therefore be built as swappable adapters, each with a health check and a fallback.

## The six required hosts need seven more to deliver data

The user's six hosts are front doors rather than data endpoints. NewsWeb's page is a JavaScript shell that loads its data from `api3.oslo.oslobors.no`; FI's insider register is served from `marknadssok.fi.se`; Finanstilsynet's short register lives on `ssr.finanstilsynet.no`; MFN's official per-issuer feeds and attachments sit on `feed.mfn.se` and `storage.mfn.se`; and the standard yfinance client fetches history from `query2.finance.yahoo.com` ([yfinance issue 2377](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2377)), using `fc.yahoo.com` for its cookie. **No data endpoint below could be live-tested during the research itself**, so each specification rests on open-source code and dated third-party tests. Once network access was opened, the core endpoints of all six collectors were tested live on 2 October 2026; the addendum at the end records what held and corrects what did not. All six collectors should share one design: an identifying User-Agent with a contact address, polite pacing with backoff, a raw-payload archive keyed by SHA-256 that records both the source's publication time and the app's first-seen time, schema validation that fails loudly, and a nightly reconciliation against a second source where one exists ([Insynshandel config](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ErikMalmgren/Insynshandel/HEAD/src/insynshandel/config.py)).

### Oslo Børs NewsWeb is the Norwegian backbone

NewsWeb is where Norwegian issuers' inside information, reports, insider trades, flagging and buyback reports appear first, around the clock. Its single-page app reads its API base from `urls.json` and calls a keyless JSON API under `/v1/newsreader` ([obs-react](https://github.com/Hallis1221/obs-react/blob/e7c88b1098fba5d201fd3256fe3f57421ed9082e/investigate_api_final.py)). At least seven independent 2026 projects poll it, and one GitHub Action parsed insider trades through it on **1 October 2026** ([innsider data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remebjornatle/innsider/main/docs/data.json)). An audit that tried a different host found in the JavaScript bundle, `obns-api.euronext.cloud`, could not resolve it ([marketscan audit](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/datatest-publik-norden.md)), so the collector should rediscover the base URL at every start instead of hard-coding it. Twenty numeric categories (1001–1302) already label most trading-relevant events ([bjelle-ai](https://github.com/kennyng90/bjelle-ai/blob/6543f81ccc3dc04771262c18927715390d9d882a/apps/workers/src/source/newsweb.ts)), and correction links in both directions make revisions easy to supersede.

| Aspect | Specification |
|---|---|
| Hosts | `newsweb.oslobors.no` (config and human-readable `/message/<id>` links); `api3.oslo.oslobors.no` (all data) |
| Discovery | `GET https://newsweb.oslobors.no/urls.json` at startup; use `api_large` as the base, falling back to `https://api3.oslo.oslobors.no` |
| Endpoints | `GET {base}/v1/newsreader/list?fromDate=YYYY-MM-DD&toDate=YYYY-MM-DD` with optional `category`, `issuer`, `messageTitle` (POST with an empty body also works); `GET /message?messageId=`; `GET /attachment?messageId=&attachmentId=`; `POST /issuers` with body `{}`; `GET /categories`; `GET /markets`. Send `Accept: application/json`, `Origin: https://newsweb.oslobors.no` and a matching `Referer` |
| Fields to keep | `messageId`, `newsId`, `title`, `category[].id`, `markets[]`, `issuerId`, `issuerSign`, `issuerName`, `publishedTime` (UTC, milliseconds), `clientAnnouncementId`, `numbAttachments`, `correctionForMessageId`, `correctedByMessageId`; from `/message`, `body` and `attachments[{id,name}]`; from `/issuers`, `issuerId`, `name`, `issuerSign`, `isActive` |

The open questions in that table are real conflicts, not formalities. One client reports a 50-message cap ([siqizhu-uk](https://github.com/siqizhu-uk/case_study_supply_chain/blob/1a0edbf39add86b4ccb8050a0ca7ff307ccdab4f/pipelines/A_company_financials/src/pipeline_a/newsweb.py)), another 600 with one request per second recommended ([bjelle-ai fixtures](https://github.com/kennyng90/bjelle-ai/blob/main/apps/workers/test/fixtures/newsweb/LESMEG.md)), a third 601 ([obs-react](https://github.com/Hallis1221/obs-react/blob/e7c88b1098fba5d201fd3256fe3f57421ed9082e/investigate_api_final.py)); one says `issuer` must be the numeric ID ([SAB](https://github.com/NicolaiBaklund/SAB/blob/be4b5a7e479a9d80370ecbca9eb5d34e8df61579/docs/data-sources.md)) and another that the issuer filter does not work at all ([STB](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sindre31/STB/HEAD/scripts/update-data.mjs)). Pulling by date and filtering client-side sidesteps both. Norwegian insider notices need the most care: bodies are free text in mixed number formats, often reading only "Please see attachment" ([finance-archive](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SkeieDK/finance-archive/HEAD/narrative/NHY.OL/2026-09.json)), and regex extraction recovers insider names only about **75%** of the time ([innsider notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remebjornatle/innsider/main/CLAUDE.md)). The collector should download attachments for 1102 notices, extract against a schema that mirrors the MAR notification template, and send low-confidence rows to a review queue. A search of open-source code found no LLM-based parser for these notices.

### Finansinspektionen supplies Sweden's insider, short and fund data

FI is the richest single Nordic source, but its insider register is served from `marknadssok.fi.se`, not `www.fi.se`. Notifications are published automatically on filing, and the register held **166,978 records on 28 August 2026**, when a third-party check found both the HTML search and the CSV export answering HTTP 200 ([marketscan reconciliation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/HEAD/.opencode/audit/insider-reconciliation-2026-08-28.md)). At least four independent codebases use the same undocumented export ([civictechsweden](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/civictechsweden/oppna-insynsregistret/main/fetch_insynsregistret.py); [Insynshandel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ErikMalmgren/Insynshandel/main/src/insynshandel/sources/fi.py); [nexus ADR](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lassemand/nexus/main/docs/adr/0002-fi-pdmr-register-access.md); [go-finansinspektionen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnteWall/go-finansinspektionen/HEAD/pkg/insider/client.go)), and a CC0 mirror on GitHub, updated nightly through 1 October 2026, is a ready fallback ([oppna-insynsregistret](https://github.com/civictechsweden/oppna-insynsregistret)). The short-position files and quarterly fund holdings live on `www.fi.se` itself ([candlechart](https://github.com/boeriksson/candlechart/blob/main/NEWS_SOURCES.md); [fondjamforaren](https://github.com/cyberw/fondjamforaren/blob/a8e388671a7773b6a628df3da202803a1e670a82/README.md)). Flagging notices, which FI publishes by noon on the trading day after receipt, are easiest to collect through MFN ([FI Börsinformation](https://www.fi.se/sv/vara-register/borsinformation/)).

| Aspect | Specification |
|---|---|
| Hosts | `www.fi.se` (short files, fund holdings, rules); `marknadssok.fi.se` (insider CSV); keep `finansinspektionen.se` allowed in case of a domain move |

Each parsing rule comes from the field semantics. Tilldelning rows are 54% share-programme awards and 45% priced at zero ([Insynshandel nature map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ErikMalmgren/Insynshandel/main/data/seed/nature_map.csv)), so counting them as purchases would swamp the signal. The JSON pitfall is documented in the same audit that verified the export ([marketscan reconciliation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/HEAD/.opencode/audit/insider-reconciliation-2026-08-28.md)). The pacing figures come from Insynshandel's production settings ([Insynshandel config](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ErikMalmgren/Insynshandel/HEAD/src/insynshandel/config.py)) and an audit that saw connection resets after seven or eight rapid requests ([marketscan audit](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/datatest-publik-norden.md)). The aggregate short file listed about **1,785 issuers and 238 named positions** on 21 September 2026 ([candlechart](https://github.com/boeriksson/candlechart/blob/main/NEWS_SOURCES.md)), and its absence-is-not-zero caveat matters because positions below 0.1% are never reported ([Dividend-Lab](https://github.com/Nils-henrik/Dividend-Lab/blob/26074ff02b9b10a39d8634450b054db67b821420/lib/companies/data-parity.ts)). The fund archive starts in Q4 2018, lags about two months and excludes foreign funds sold in Sweden ([free-data audit](https://github.com/ingencopycat/nolltillmiljoner/blob/main/docs/internal/free-data-audit-2026-09-22/report.md)); one March 2026 file unpacked into 706 fund XML files ([fondjamforaren](https://github.com/cyberw/fondjamforaren/blob/a8e388671a7773b6a628df3da202803a1e670a82/README.md)). FI permits reuse with attribution ([FI open data](https://www.fi.se/sv/om-fi/om-webbplatsen/oppen-data/)).

### Finanstilsynet contributes the short-sale API and the rule changes

Finanstilsynet's main site publishes rules, hearings and enforcement rather than data. Norwegian insiders report trades on Altinn form KRT-1500, but only issuers publish them, on NewsWeb ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/tilsyn/markedsatferd/meldeplikt-for-personer-med-ledelsesansvar/)). The data host is `ssr.finanstilsynet.no`, the only one of the six with a published OpenAPI specification: an unauthenticated "public shorting history" endpoint captured in July 2026 ([ssr-openapi.json](https://github.com/trymhaak/open-apis/blob/main/raw/finanstilsynet/2026-07-08/ssr-openapi.json)) that returned about 1.4 MB of JSON covering 98 instruments in a June 2026 probe ([open-apis manifest](https://github.com/trymhaak/open-apis/blob/main/raw/finanstilsynet/2026-06-25/manifest.json)). Positions of at least 0.1% of share capital must be reported by 15:30 on the next trading day, and those of at least 0.5% are made public ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/en/supervision/market-conduct/short-selling-and-reporting-of-short-positions/)).

| Endpoints | `GET https://ssr.finanstilsynet.no/api/v2/instruments` (all public history); `GET /api/v2/instruments/export-csv?separator=;&locale=nb-NO`; `GET /api/v2/instruments/export-json`; specification at `/api/v2/openapi.json`. The two-year retention and the 15:30 update come from Finanstilsynet's open-data page and catalogue entry ([open-apis catalog](https://github.com/trymhaak/open-apis/blob/main/catalog/by-provider/finanstilsynet/shortsalregister.yaml)); an existing collector polls at 16:00 on weekdays and writes daily snapshots ([doffin-parser registry](https://github.com/sondreskarsten/doffin-parser/blob/3c267cf00b75ea08cb5699fd87ef3eb937fc6d04/tests/registry.yaml)). The `www` collector is a low-frequency rule monitor that alerts on terms such as "flagg", "primærinnsider", "short" and "ESAP". It matters because the changes most likely to break other collectors will be announced there first: the flagging-publication transfer under consultation ([Finanstilsynet hearing](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/nyhetsarkiv/horinger/2026/horing-overforing-av-oppgaven-med-mottak-og-offentliggjoring-av-flaggemeldinger-fra-oslo-bors-til)), a possible new insider threshold, and the EU single access point, which will carry MAR insider data from 10 January 2028 at the earliest ([marketscan reconciliation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/HEAD/.opencode/audit/insider-reconciliation-2026-08-28.md)). The same site states that the MAR rules on investment recommendations apply to private persons ([Finanstilsynet 2022](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/nyhetsarkiv/nyheter/2022/finanstilsynet-presiserer-at-reglene-for-investeringsanbefalinger-ogsa-gjelder-for-privatpersoner/)), which becomes relevant if the app's output is ever shared.

### Nordnet's website yields owner counts and the cost model

Nordnet plays three roles. It is the execution venue, where customers get free streaming Level 1 quotes for Norwegian shares, so manual orders need no paid feed ([Nordnet price list](https://www.nordnet.no/kundeservice/prisliste)). It is the source of the cost model. And it is a retail-sentiment source. Its official External API is no use here: Nordnet's documentation says it is not onboarding new customers ([Nordnet API](https://www.nordnet.se/externalapi/docs/getting_started)), Nordnet's FAQ says it offers no API to private persons ([Nordnet FAQ](https://www.nordnet.se/faq/handel-vardepapper/handelsapplikationer/erbjuder-nordnet-api)), and a key grants full trading rights with no read-only option ([nordnet-api crate](https://docs.rs/nordnet-api/latest/nordnet_api/)). The practical path is the unauthenticated JSON the website itself calls, which returns per-stock owner counts, prices, market caps and key ratios, plus holder counts for bull and bear certificates on each underlying ([stockpicker](https://github.com/ryddmo/stockpicker); [NordnetAktieScreener](https://github.com/FlemmingJahn/NordnetAktieScreener); [StocksPlatform](https://github.com/jon-tj/StocksPlatform)).

| Aspect | Specification |
|---|---|

A daily-history path ([TradingBot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/theBadMusician/TradingBot/master/Scrapers/NordnetScraper.py)) and trade-tape and market-data paths ([nordnet-sdk](https://github.com/luimu64/nordnet-sdk)) are unverified. The bull/bear weighting is one project's design, not established evidence ([StocksPlatform](https://github.com/jon-tj/StocksPlatform)). Owner counts move daily at an undocumented time, so store `statistics_timestamp` and treat each count as available only after it. Nordnet history begins the day the collector does; only Avanza's owner-history endpoint can backfill this signal ([avanza-ts](https://github.com/AnteWall/avanza-ts)). Nordnet's monthly statistics name the most net-bought stocks, Kongsberg Gruppen in September 2026 ([Nordnet via Cision](https://news.cision.com/no/nordnet/r/aksjestatistikk-september--nordnet-kundene-okte-aksjeeksponeringen-betydelig-i-en-turbulent-maned---,c4402480)), but they cover only about ten names after month-end, so they serve better as labels than as signals. The same `www.nordnet.no` collector should also read the price list, the currency-exchange FAQ and the venue list, which feed the cost model and the eligibility flags: Euronext Growth shares, for example, cannot be held in a Norwegian ASK ([Nordnet](https://www.nordnet.no/blogg/hvorfor-kan-jeg-ikke-ha-euronext-growth-aksjer-pa-min-aksjesparekonto)).

### MFN covers every Nordic issuer but discourages bulk JSON

MFN offers press releases and history "for all listed companies in the Nordic region, regardless of provider" ([MFN](https://mfn.se/about)), with ISINs, tickers, tags and full text inline. All 20 items checked in one live sample carried an ISIN, and issuers routinely file the same release twice, locally and in English, linked by `group_id` ([wsbg-terminal](https://github.com/bsommerfeld/wsbg-terminal/blob/4cfca13cf50fa223ca27a523055537dd536869c1/web/impl/src/main/java/de/bsommerfeld/wsbg/terminal/web/impl/sources/briefing/MfnDisclosureClient.java)). That makes MFN the best single source for Swedish events and a cross-check for Norwegian, Danish and Finnish ones. It also timestamps interim reports precisely enough to serve as the availability clock for point-in-time fundamentals ([marketscan report](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md)). The catch is a clear terms signal: **mfn.se's robots.txt disallows `*.json`, `*.rss`, `*.xml` and `*.atom` for every agent** ([ai-fund-manager](https://github.com/ACWesterberg/ai-fund-manager/blob/b64862fb2461c16437d34bc649fd82f6b7eee74e/docs/MONITORING.md)).

| Aspect | Specification |
|---|---|
| Hosts | `mfn.se` (aggregate); `feed.mfn.se` (per-issuer feeds, archive, WebSub hub); `storage.mfn.se` (attachments) |
| Aggregate | `GET https://mfn.se/all/a.json?limit=100&offset=0`, following `next_url` until reaching the last stored `news_id`; `limit` up to 200 |
| Special streams | `GET https://mfn.se/all/a/fi-se.json?limit=200` (FI flagging notices); `GET https://mfn.se/all/s/nordic.json?limit=300` (Nordic stream, used for bid detection) |

Swedish buyback execution tables can be parsed into date, shares, average price and transaction value, as one tracker does for Evolution ([evodata](https://github.com/evuul/evodata/blob/e52d7884786a4bb4e98f5969218d9b98099858e9/src/lib/buybacksSync.js)). **Because the app is a web application with a public HTTPS address, it can use MFN's WebSub hub**, which pushes new items for subscribed issuers within seconds and is the consumption path MFN built for issuer websites ([ai-fund-manager](https://github.com/ACWesterberg/ai-fund-manager/blob/b64862fb2461c16437d34bc649fd82f6b7eee74e/docs/MONITORING.md)). Subscribing to watchlist issuers and polling the aggregate gently is both faster and more defensible than polling `a.json` every minute.

### Yahoo's chart API carries prices, futures and outcomes

Yahoo is the only free source covering all four Nordic markets, including Euronext Growth Oslo and First North. A third-party test on 28 August 2026 fetched ten years of gap-free daily history and fresh quarterly fundamentals for ten Nordic tickers ([marketscan report](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md)), and yfinance, the client most projects use, reached version 1.7.0 on 26 August 2026. Its terms are the binding limit: the project reminds users that "the Yahoo! finance API is intended for personal use only" ([yfinance on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/yfinance/)).

### A shared clock and event map tie the collectors together

The collectors run on the exchanges' clock, not a fixed interval. Stockholm opens with an auction at 09:00, trades continuously until 17:25 and closes with an auction at 17:30 ([Nasdaq Nordic Market Model](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/2025/09/29/Nasdaq_Nordic_Market_Model_2025_04.pdf)). Oslo's closing timetable is disputed: one Euronext guide gives continuous trading to 16:20 and a closing auction uncrossing around 16:25 ([Euronext guide](https://connect2.euronext.com/sites/default/files/it-documentation/Guide%20to%20New%20Trading%20System%20-%20issue%203%200.pdf)), while a broker lists trading to 16:25 and an auction to 16:30 ([DEGIRO](https://www.degiro.com/uk/products-and-markets/exchange-opening-times)). SSB publishes the weekly salmon export price on Wednesdays at 08:00 ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/en/utenriksokonomi/statistikker/laks)). The Riksbank announces decisions at 09:30 on the day after its meeting ([Riksbank](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/monetary-policy/monetary-policy-report/2026/monetary-policy-decision-september-2026/)) and Norges Bank at 10:00, with remaining 2026 decisions reported for 5 November and 17 December in a secondary calendar ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/topics/monetary-policy/Monetary-policy-meetings/2026/may-2026/); [economic-atlas](https://github.com/ciaranbelcher10/economic-atlas)). Avanza's owner counts update between 18:00 and 19:00 ([stockpicker](https://github.com/ryddmo/stockpicker)), and Börsdata's end-of-day data at about 20:00 ([Börsdata wiki](https://github.com/Borsdata-Sweden/API/wiki/Stockprice)).

| Local time (CET/CEST) | Collector action | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 06:30–18:30 weekdays | NewsWeb every 60 s; MFN every 2 min; FI insider register every 15 min | Releases at 07:00–08:00 are priced in the 09:00 opening auction |
| 08:00 Wednesday | SSB salmon export price, table 03024 | Weekly pre-open event for seafood names |
| 08:50 | Yahoo snapshot of `ES=F`, `NQ=F`, `^VIX`, `^TNX`, `DX-Y.NYB`, `BZ=F`, `TTF=F` | Predicts the opening gap |
| 09:30 or 10:00 on decision days | Riksbank or Norges Bank decision | Banks, real estate, NOK and SEK reprice during the session |
| Until 13:00 | FI flagging notices via MFN `fi-se` | FI publishes by noon on the next trading day |
| 16:00 | Finanstilsynet SSR snapshot; FI short files | Both registers update around 15:30 |
| 16:20–16:30 and 17:25–17:30 | Oslo and Stockholm closing auctions; second snapshot of global tickers | Closing prices anchor outcomes |
| 18:00–19:00 | Avanza owner counts | Treated as next-day inputs |
| 19:00–21:00 | Nordnet stock list; Yahoo end-of-day bars; Börsdata after its 20:00 update | End-of-day features and the prediction run |
| Night | FI 90-day insider re-scan; NewsWeb D−7 reconciliation; outcome scoring | Revisions and evaluation |
| Weekly | Universe and identifier maps; NewsWeb issuer list; FI fund-holdings page; Finanstilsynet rule monitor | Slow reference data |
| Quarterly, annually | FI fund holdings, about two months after quarter end; Skatteetaten shareholder register in May | Long-sleeve ownership data |

The event map turns each source's native labels into one taxonomy, so a Norwegian buyback and a Swedish one land in the same feature. NewsWeb's category IDs and Nasdaq's category strings carry most of the classification for free, MFN tags carry the rest, and only ambiguous inside-information and press-release items need an LLM ([bjelle-ai](https://github.com/kennyng90/bjelle-ai/blob/6543f81ccc3dc04771262c18927715390d9d882a/apps/workers/src/source/newsweb.ts); [marketscan news events](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/backend_worker/news_events.py); [mfn-wp-plugin](https://github.com/modfin/mfn-wp-plugin/blob/2f6d07fb1bbf71de51b966cfeef1aabcdc838541/lib.php)).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interim or annual report | 1001, 1002, 1003 | `sub:report:interim`, `sub:report:annual`; "Interim report", "Quarterly report" | Rules; LLM for key figures | Short: event window. Long: fundamentals timestamp |

Six one-request checks decide whether these specifications hold, and they should run before any backfill. They test whether NewsWeb truncates at 50, 600 or 601 messages and what `issuer=` accepts; whether FI's short files are still OpenDocument and still on `www.fi.se` rather than `finansinspektionen.se` ([FI register page](https://finansinspektionen.se/sv/vara-register/blankningsregistret/emittent/?id=549300LZSA4S0L54VQ25)); whether Finanstilsynet's `shortPercent` counts only public positions, and under which licence; which stock-list parameters Nordnet accepts; whether MFN honours tag filters and what page size it returns; and whether Yahoo's v8 chart answers on `query1` without a cookie, and with what quote delay.

## Sixty-seven more hosts complete the allow-list

Six sources cannot cover Danish and Finnish announcements, point-in-time fundamentals, consensus estimates, rates or commodities. The most valuable additions are Börsdata Pro+, Nasdaq Nordic's public endpoints, Euronext's history CSV, Avanza's owner history, the central-bank and statistics APIs, and a few commodity feeds.

**Börsdata Pro+** costs 599 SEK or €59 a month and has been the only tier with REST access since 1 February 2025 ([Börsdata prices](https://borsdata.se/en/pricetable); [Börsdata API](https://github.com/Borsdata-Sweden/API/blob/master/README.md)). Through `https://apiservice.borsdata.se/v1/` with an `authKey` query parameter it serves up to 20 years of end-of-day prices, 20 annual and 40 quarterly reports stamped with a `report_Date`, KPI histories, report and dividend calendars, and structured `/v1/holdings/insider`, `/v1/holdings/shorts` and `/v1/holdings/buyback` data for Nordic companies, though its Norwegian coverage should be confirmed with a trial. The limits are 100 calls per 10 seconds and fewer than 10,000 a day ([Börsdata wiki](https://github.com/Borsdata-Sweden/API/wiki); [swagger copy](https://github.com/emmanuelay/borsdata-mcp/blob/54efe9d021a2fe23168a2e4db2e571fdd95e4a8f/client/assets/swagger.json); [PyFinModel](https://github.com/carlwestman/PyFinModel/blob/aee035bdb0263105b230eff2f6fda04c937ef1d7/PyFinModeler/data/borsdata_collector.py)). It has no consensus endpoint, its documentation is silent on delisted companies and on whether reports are restated, and its licence reportedly limits use to a private person's own analysis and forbids external systems that display API data ([marketscan landscape](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/nordic-data-landscape.md)). That suits a single-user app, not a shared one. Treat `report_Date` plus one day as the availability time, and backtests built on it as upper bounds.

**Nasdaq Nordic's** public website API returns the Stockholm, Copenhagen and Helsinki universe in one GET, `https://api.nasdaq.com/api/nordic/screener/shares?category=MAIN_MARKET&tableonly=true&page=1&size=1000&lang=en` (with `FIRST_NORTH` for growth markets; its firewall blocks POST). In August 2026 it returned 411 Stockholm main-market rows and 332 First North rows ([marketscan audit](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/.opencode/audit/datatest-publik-norden.md)), and `/api/nordic/instruments/{orderbookId}/chart?assetClass=SHARES&fromDate=&toDate=&lang=en` gives daily bars ([Elis](https://github.com/JanRou/Elis/blob/5379bb06ddb2103ea587030444fc0856ec21b0e8/GetStockPrizes/Exchanges/NasdaqOmxClient.py); [screener](https://github.com/dagulv/screener/blob/f61d0d58165b7037faa625667eeb2d9c60970faa/internal/adapter/scraper/scraper.go)). Its company-news service, `https://api.news.eu.nasdaq.com/news/query.action?type=json&market=…&cnscategory=…&limit=…&start=…`, carries uniform categories such as "Managers' Transactions" and "Inside information" for every Nasdaq Nordic issuer ([StonksBot](https://github.com/etsubu/StonksBot/blob/070a27b00cffe39be14af724be7a614476f817d3/src/main/java/com/etsubu/stonksbot/scheduler/omxnordic/NasdaqNordicDisclosures.java)). That makes it the natural source of Danish and Finnish insider trades, which neither country publishes as a structured register ([bloomvalley](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/freducom/bloomvalley/HEAD/backend/app/pipelines/nasdaq_nordic_insider.py); [Finanstilsynet DK](https://www.finanstilsynet.dk/finansielle-temaer/kapitalmarked/mar-for-ledende-medarbejdere)). The obstacle is that **Nasdaq's website terms forbid automated capture**, which one 2026 project treated as disqualifying ([Dividend-Lab](https://github.com/Nils-henrik/Dividend-Lab/blob/26074ff02b9b10a39d8634450b054db67b821420/lib/companies/source-policy.ts)); polling more often than every 30 seconds is discouraged regardless ([TradingAgents-Finnish](https://github.com/Murtsi/TradingAgents-Finnish/blob/562b0d2ec4bf7d632690441420e74d0c31fc5aff/tradingagents/dataflows/finnish_news.py)).

**Euronext's** undocumented CSV, `https://live.euronext.com/en/ajax/AwlHistoricalPrice/getFullDownloadAjax/{ISIN}-{MIC}?format=csv&decimal_separator=.&date_form=d/m/Y&adjusted=Y&startdate=YYYY-MM-DD&enddate=YYYY-MM-DD`, is the Oslo price cross-check for the three Oslo venues (`XOSL`, `XOAS`, `MERK`) and was capped at two years per request in a July 2026 test ([Proeftuin-go](https://github.com/ishiyan/Proeftuin-go/blob/ae2b9ab5479338a37e40a80b2fec93ea47d55ab7/csv/euronext/euronext/euronext.go); [Auto-Quant-V2](https://github.com/TraderAlice/Auto-Quant-V2/blob/52d63148d826e6c35d48c3167d95a4cc7a4eb6c4/.claude/skills/fetch-euronext-ohlcv/references/provider-route.md)). Downloading both `adjusted=Y` and `adjusted=N` should expose Euronext's own cumulative adjustment factors, which is the cheapest way to catch Oslo rights issues, since no free structured rights-issue feed exists; this is an untested inference. Euronext's stock-list download supplies the Oslo universe ([atlas-app](https://github.com/DavidMM-coder/atlas-app/blob/463bdf3ef2ad8fe912cd21f298307d8afb0567b6/api/_lib/scout-sources.js)), and OpenFIGI (`https://api.openfigi.com/v3/mapping`, ten jobs per request without a key) and ESMA's FIRDS files map ISINs, venues and tickers ([stockdash](https://github.com/jhalevi-blip/stockdash/blob/34c3415ee5830f3536c19ecf827ade4d723cada3/lib/brokers/isinResolver.ts); [eu_finreg_data](https://github.com/bunburya/eu_finreg_data/blob/eafecd05490a34f28ba4feb2515d2f18f80d7a3a/firds.py)). The canonical instrument key should be ISIN plus MIC plus trading currency.

**Retail and analyst data** come from Avanza and MarketScreener. Avanza's `/_api/market-guide/number-of-owners/{orderbookId}` returns owner-count history in an undocumented format, without authentication if sent with a browser User-Agent ([avanza-ts](https://github.com/AnteWall/avanza-ts); [agardata-avanza](https://github.com/psykopotatis/agardata-avanza)), though Avanza's customer terms also reportedly forbid systematic extraction ([stockpicker](https://github.com/ryddmo/stockpicker)). MarketScreener's free consensus page, `https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/{NAME}-{ID}/consensus/`, shows mean, median, high and low targets and buy/hold/sell counts behind Cloudflare, so scrape it at 1.5-second intervals and snapshot daily to build a revision history that no free source provides ([MarketScreener notes](https://github.com/gauss314/skills/blob/5156f818420dc9098ac1071e26d34bb7294efe12/skills/marketscreener/references/ENDPOINTS.md); [marketscan report](https://github.com/hankkontakt/marketscan/blob/e5052ddca257445316be23bdc887132e8871df34/RAPPORT_DATASYSTEM_2026-08-28.md)). Other sentiment sources are thin and closing: Shareville became Nordnet Social in April 2026 ([Nordnet](https://www.nordnet.se/blogg/shareville-blir-nordnet-social/)), Reddit has required manual approval for every API client since 11 November 2025 ([redditapis](https://www.redditapis.com/reddit-responsible-builder-policy)), X charges about $0.005 per post read ([Outstand](https://www.outstand.so/blog/x-api-pricing)), and pytrends is archived ([pytrends](https://github.com/GeneralMills/pytrends)). Post counts from Finansavisen's HO forum and Avanza's Placera forum, stored as counts rather than text, are the cheapest attention proxies ([HO-forum scraper](https://github.com/11Mali/datacleaningfolder); [Placera](https://www.placera.se/telegram/placeras-nya-forum-igang-2022-12-20)).

**Rates, FX and macro** series are free and mostly keyless. Norges Bank's SDMX API serves the policy rate (`https://data.norges-bank.no/api/data/IR/B.KPRA.SD.R?format=csv`), NOWA (`SHORT_RATES/B.NOWA.ON.R`), NOK exchange rates (`EXR/B.{CCY}.NOK.SP`) and government yields (`GOVT_GENERIC_RATES/B.3Y+5Y+10Y.GBON`) ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/topics/statistics/open-data/); [shares-analytics](https://github.com/Sindre31/shares-analytics)). The Riksbank's `https://api.riksbank.se/swea/v1/Observations/{seriesId}/{from}` serves series such as `SECBREPOEFF` and `SEKNOKPMI`, at 5 calls a minute anonymously or 200 with a free key ([Riksbank](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/statistics/interest-rates-and-exchange-rates/retrieving-interest-rates-and-exchange-rates-via-api/series-for-the-api/); [Berit](https://github.com/robinandreeklund-collab/Berit)). The ECB's `EXR` dataflow ([ECB](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/help/api/overview)), FRED with a free key ([FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/)), and SSB's and SCB's PxWebApi v2, both launched in autumn 2025, complete the set ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/en/api/pxwebapi); [SCB](https://www.scb.se/en/services/open-data-api/pxwebapi/pxwebapi-2.0)). Norway's manufacturing PMI was discontinued after its 1 December 2025 release ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/norway/manufacturing-pmi/news/506310)), and Sweden's Silf/Swedbank PMI has no API.

**Commodity drivers** need Yahoo futures for anything pre-open, because the EIA Brent series, as mirrored on FRED, still ended on 29 September when checked on 2 October 2026 ([ALFRED](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/series?seid=DCOILBRENTEU)); EIA's `https://api.eia.gov/v2/petroleum/pri/spt/data/` with a free key is for clean daily history ([oil](https://github.com/scottstanfield/oil)). SSB table 03024, fetched through `https://data.ssb.no/api/pxwebapi/v2/tables/03024/data?lang=en&…`, gives the weekly salmon price before the Oslo open ([PxTools](https://github.com/PxTools/pxtools.github.io)). ENTSO-E's free-token API returns day-ahead power prices for bidding zones NO1–NO5 and SE1–SE4, at 15-minute resolution since 1 October 2025 and up to 400 requests a minute ([entsoe-quickstart](https://github.com/whipeeer-creator/entsoe-quickstart); [ECTL](https://github.com/maurorisso/ECTL)), with keyless mirrors at hvakosterstrommen.no and elprisetjustnu.se ([Raycast extensions](https://github.com/raycast/extensions)). Nord Pool's official API costs EUR 2,700 per region per year ([api-evangelist](https://github.com/api-evangelist/nordpool)), and LBMA requires a licence to obtain or use its gold benchmark ([LBMA](https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/lbma-gold-price)), so Yahoo's `GC=F` is the practical gold series. Index calendars belong here too: OBX and OSEBX changes take effect after the third Friday of March and September ([OBX rulebook](https://live.euronext.com/sites/default/files/documentation/index-rules2/OBX_Index_Family_Rulebook.pdf)), and OMXS30 changes on the first trading day of January and July, selected by free-float market cap since 1 July 2025 ([OMXS30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OMX_Stockholm_30)).

**True intraday data** is the one input with no free licensed route. Nasdaq's non-professional Level 1 fee is only €1.13 a month but must come through a licensed distributor ([Nasdaq price list](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/Nasdaq_European_Exchange_Market_Data_Price_List_January_2026)); Saxo's Nordic Level 1 starts at €47 for professionals ([Saxo](https://www.home.saxo/products/market-data-subscriptions)); and Millistream's 549 SEK/month Trader plan covers Sweden, Denmark and Finland but apparently not Norway ([SaaSBrowser](https://saasbrowser.com/en/saas/542379/millistream)). Fifteen-minute-delayed and end-of-day data suffice for one-day to two-week horizons, and both exchange groups treat internal use of delayed data as free ([Euronext](https://www.euronext.com/en/data/market-data/market-data-pricing-policies); [Nasdaq data policies](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/Nasdaq_European_Data_Policies_January_2026_New)). For backtests, no free survivorship-free, point-in-time Nordic stock dataset exists. JKP's country factor files (CC BY-NC 4.0, in USD) ([tidy-finance](https://github.com/tidy-finance/r-tidyfinance/blob/main/R/download_data_jkp.R)), AQR's quality and betting-against-beta country columns ([ml4t](https://github.com/ml4t/data/blob/main/src/ml4t/data/providers/aqr.py)), Ødegaard's Norwegian factors from 1981 ([Quandl metadata](https://github.com/jimmoores/quandl4j/blob/7331289a57980dfd891eadf674948f355e143f1d/core/src/test/resources/com/jimmoores/quandl/tests/testresults/MetaData46.json)) and French's Norway and Sweden value portfolios from 1975 ([French](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/int_index_port_formed.html)) calibrate theme-level priors. A home-grown stock-level backtest that beats them by a wide margin should be presumed biased.

Website terms, not exchange fees, are the binding legal constraint for a personal app. Euronext requires no licence for non-display use of delayed data, and Nasdaq says delayed Nordic data is generally free ([Euronext](https://www.euronext.com/en/data/market-data/market-data-pricing-policies); [Nasdaq data policies](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/Nasdaq_European_Data_Policies_January_2026_New)). By contrast, Yahoo is personal-use only, MFN's robots.txt and Nasdaq's site terms discourage automated capture, Nordnet's and Avanza's customer terms reportedly forbid systematic extraction, and Börsdata forbids external display. A single-user app that polls slowly, caches, records provenance and never republishes prices stays within the spirit of all of them. The moment it is shared, Yahoo, MarketScreener and the undocumented exchange endpoints have to be replaced by licensed feeds. Insider and short-holder names are personal data even in public registers, so store them as salted hashes plus a role class and keep them out of logs.

## Nordnet costs, not data, decide which signals survive

### A round trip costs 0.5% to 2% before any edge

Nordnet's minimum fees, not its percentages, dominate small orders. In Norway the Mini class charges 0.15% with a **NOK 29 minimum** on Nordic shares, so any order below about NOK 19,300 pays more than 0.15% ([Nordnet price list](https://www.nordnet.no/kundeservice/prisliste); [Nordinnsikt](https://nordinnsikt.no/meglere/nordnet/)). In Sweden, Mini charges 0.25% with a SEK 1 minimum up to SEK 15,600; Liten charges 0.15%, Mellan 0.069%, and a flat SEK 99 class applies above about SEK 143,000 ([Nordnet prislista](https://www.nordnet.se/kundservice/prislista); [Kvalitetsaktier](https://kvalitetsaktier.se/courtage-jamforelse)). Crossing the spread adds roughly one quoted spread per round trip, which cross-country data put at 17 basis points for large stocks, 41 for medium and **148 for small** ([BIS](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1229.pdf)). A NOK account buying Stockholm shares also pays 0.25% on each automatic currency conversion, or 0.075% through a currency account ([Nordnet FX](https://www.nordnet.no/faq/priser/valuta-og-veksling/automatisk-veksling/hvordan-fungerer-valutaveksling-pa-nordnet)).

| Order | Nordnet class | Round-trip courtage | With large-cap spread | With small-cap spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEK 10,000 | Mini, 0.25% | 0.50% | ≈0.67% | ≈2.0% |
| SEK 30,000 | Liten, 0.15% | 0.30% | ≈0.47% | ≈1.8% |
| SEK 100,000 | Mellan, 0.069% | 0.14% | ≈0.31% | ≈1.6% |
| NOK 10,000 | Mini, NOK 29 minimum | 0.58% | ≈0.75% | ≈2.1% |
| NOK 20,000 | Mini, 0.15% | 0.30% | ≈0.47% | ≈1.8% |
| NOK 100,000 | Normal, NOK 79 minimum | 0.16% | ≈0.33% | ≈1.6% |

Sources disagree on some tiers: a NOK 39 rather than 29 Norwegian minimum, 0.10% rather than 0.049% for Norway's Normal class, and SEK 49 rather than 39 for Liten. The cost model should therefore read the live price list monthly instead of hard-coding it, and promotions such as Sweden's courtage-free offer for new customers until 30 June 2027 should stay out of backtests ([Nordnet prislista](https://www.nordnet.se/kundservice/prislista)). Taxes widen the gap outside tax wrappers: realised Norwegian gains are taxed at 37.84%, while Sweden's ISK taxes capital rather than trades, at an effective 1.065% above SEK 300,000 in 2026, which makes frequent trading tax-neutral inside ISK and the Norwegian ASK ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/vardepapper/investeringssparkontoisk.4.5fc8c94513259a4ba1d800037851.html); [AksjeNorge](https://aksjenorge.no/aksjesparekonto/)). Euronext Growth shares stay outside the ASK unless a proposal to admit them from 1 January 2027 is enacted ([Regjeringen](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/horing-utvidet-aksjesparekonto-vil-gi-flere-investeringsmuligheter/id3167178/)).

At these prices, turnover decides survival as much as signal quality does. Two round trips a week at 0.6% each consume about 60% of traded capital a year. A NOK 40,000 sleeve split into four NOK 10,000 positions that makes 150 round trips a year spends about NOK 8,700, or 22% of the sleeve, on courtage alone, before any spread. Tick sizes add a floor of their own: under the MiFID II tick regime, a stock priced at 2.00 with a 0.01 tick has a one-tick spread of 50 basis points ([RTS 11](https://ec.europa.eu/finance/securities/docs/isd/mifid/rts/160714-rts-11_en.pdf)). The app should exclude any instrument where one tick exceeds the signal's expected edge.

### Most short-horizon edges sit below the hurdle

Against a 0.5–0.75% hurdle in liquid names and about 2% in small caps, almost every documented short-horizon signal falls short once costs and the retail investor's late place in the information chain are counted.

| Signal | Best evidence | Gross effect and horizon | Against the hurdle | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh insider purchases | Helsinki 2005–2010 ([Aalto](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/handle/123456789/499)) | +0.25% over 5 days | Below | Confirmation only |
| Buyback announcement | Stockholm 2000–2009 ([Råsbrant](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1780967)); Oslo 1998–2001 ([Skjeltorp](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228391537_The_market_impact_and_timing_of_open_market_share_repurchases_in_Norway)) | ≈+2% over 2 days; +2.52% over days −2 to +2, with under-reaction | Partly; much accrues on day 0 | Test next-open entry |
| New or larger disclosed short | EU-wide disclosures ([Jones, Reed & Waller](https://econpapers.repec.org/article/ouprfinst/v_3a29_3ay_3a2016_3ai_3a12_3ap_3a3278-3320..htm)) | −1.78% over 3 days; −5.23% over 90 days | Large, but the account is long-only | Avoid or exit |
| Quarterly report | Oslo 2013–2016 ([CBS](https://research.cbs.dk/da/studentProjects/ef598439-563b-45f1-9c6d-3853655cdbc9/)) | Reaction on days 0 and +1, no drift | Retail arrives late | Event-window flag |
| Intraday momentum | 16 developed markets ([Li, Sakkas & Urquhart](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S138641812100001X)); Stockholm ([DiVA](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1878991/FULLTEXT01.pdf)) | Basis points; negative out-of-sample R² for OMXS30 | Below | Log only |
| Short-term reversal | Nordics 1995–2022, monthly ([Aalto](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/items/bf3e55af-8821-4dd7-a168-72e458958557)) | Positive before costs, concentrated in illiquid names | Below in small caps | Log only |
| Trend signals | Investtech, Nordics 1996–2018, vendor study ([Investtech](https://www.investtech.com/us/market.php?CountryID=993&fn=wpArticle&p=staticPage&tbReport=tbib_rising_2019art)) | +0.8 points over one month, gross | About one liquid round trip | Not standalone |
| Retail attention onset | US search volume ([Da, Engelberg & Gao](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01679.x)) | Higher prices for about 2 weeks, reversal within a year | Unknown | Learn the sign first |
| Retail herding extremes | US Robinhood users ([Barber et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13183)) | −4.7% over 20 days for the most-bought stocks | Large, but long-only | Avoid flag |
| Analyst rating changes | Stockholm 2010–2014 ([SSE](http://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=3018)) | +1.58% over 3 days | Brokers trade first | Very low weight |
| Overnight oil and US moves | Oslo ([Bjørnland](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-9485.2009.00482.x)) | A 10% oil rise lifts stocks about 2.5% | Priced at the open | Sizing and risk filter |

Two signals are worth testing with real fills. Buyback announcements earn about 2% over two days in Stockholm, and in Oslo the market under-reacted and prices drew further support on actual repurchase days, so an entry at the next opening auction might keep part of the effect. New or larger disclosed short positions preceded about −2.75% over ten days and −5.23% over 90 days in EU-wide data, which in a long-only account means "do not buy, and review holdings". Everything else should be logged, not traded. UK directors' purchases earn roughly zero net of the spread ([directors-dealings notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rspiegelberg-cmd/directors-dealings/HEAD/docs/research/alpha-research-2026-06-10.md)). A study of technical rules on the Helsinki, Oslo and Stockholm exchanges found them highly sensitive to costs, with higher simulated costs turning short-sale returns negative ([Vaasa](https://core.ac.uk/download/pdf/197964347.pdf)). A Stockholm study of broker-level data found that recommending brokers trade profitably around their own positive revisions, suggesting the information reaches their order flow before the public ([Anderson & Martinez](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2019072)). Retail-attention signals cut both ways: the onset of attention lifts prices briefly, while extreme herding precedes losses, so the app must learn the sign from Nordic owner-count data before giving it any weight.

The prior for a retail day-trading sleeve is negative. In complete Taiwanese trading records, **fewer than 1% of day traders were predictably profitable** ([Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/The%20Cross-Section%20of%20Speculator%20Skill.pdf)), and **97% of Brazilians who day traded mini-index futures for more than 300 days lost money** ([Chague et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101)). If the user still wants the sleeve, the evidence favours two-to-ten-day holds in liquid large and mid caps over true intraday trading. Entries should follow pre-open news at the 09:00 opening auction, because releases at 07:00–08:00 are priced there, and limit orders should replace market orders. The backtest's fill model should pay half the spread per side for market orders, fill limit orders only when the price trades through, apply Nordnet's minimum fees and tick rounding, and treat volatility halts as auctions rather than stop-level fills ([Nasdaq Nordic Market Model](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/2025/09/29/Nasdaq_Nordic_Market_Model_2025_04.pdf)).

### Momentum, quality value and low volatility carry the long sleeve

Long holding periods amortise Nordnet's costs, and three factor families have Nordic-specific, peer-reviewed support. Event channels then act as overlays.

| Theme | Nordic evidence | Role in the long sleeve |
|---|---|---|
| Momentum (12-1) | Strong at every firm size in the Nordics 1993–2017, with winner-value stocks earning 1.66% a month ([Grobys & Huhta-Halkola](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3226899)); 1.19–2.43% a month across 16 Oslo strategies ([CBS thesis](https://research-api.cbs.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/58452773/kristoffer_absalonsen_og_alexander_vas.pdf)) | Core; volatility-scaled; re-ranked monthly or quarterly with entry and exit bands |
| Low risk | Idiosyncratic-volatility sorts strongest and beta sorts weakest in the Nordics 2005–2024, muted in large caps, holding up to 12 months ([Grobys, Hartikainen & Äijö](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00036846.2025.2497563)) | Exclude or penalise the most volatile quintile |
| Value | Paid only when small stocks were included ([Grobys & Huhta-Halkola](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3226899)); EBIT/EV gave the best risk-adjusted returns in Finland 1991–2013 ([Davydov, Tikkanen & Äijö](https://osuva.uwasa.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/83ce8cde-14e6-48c9-98e4-7dd3b6fe7629/content)); Oslo's value premium was negative in 1996–2016 ([CBS](https://research.cbs.dk/en/studentProjects/beae3b6c-9189-4e30-9325-35ac337875dd/)) | Composite of EBIT/EV, earnings yield and free-cash-flow yield, quality-filtered and ranked within sector |
| Quality | Quality-minus-junk pays in 24 countries ([Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-018-9470-2)); Nordic small caps skew "junky" ([Jyväskylä thesis](https://jyx.jyu.fi/jyx/Record/jyx_123456789_104086)) | Filter for value; penalise net share issuance |
| Insider net buying | Oslo insiders earned zero or negative abnormal returns in 1985–1992 ([Eckbo & Smith](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/danielyang1009/light-speed-engine/HEAD/JF/Volume%2053/Volume%2053%20-%20Issue%202.md)); Swedish insider trading is driven mostly by rebalancing, tax and bias ([Kallunki, Nilsson & Hellström](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1426899)); less wealthy Swedish insiders time their sales ([Kallunki et al.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/danielyang1009/light-speed-engine/HEAD/JFE/Volume%20130/Volume%20130%20-%20Issue%201.md)) | Modest one-to-six-month tilt; selling by several insiders as a veto |
| Buybacks | +6–7% in the 12 months after Stockholm programme initiations in 2000–2009 ([SSE thesis](http://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=6372)); long-run drift by firm size in 2005–2013 ([DiVA thesis](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1222662/FULLTEXT01.pdf)) | Positive overlay, larger for cheap stocks |
| Disclosed shorts | Positions bunch just below the 0.5% disclosure threshold and are followed by more negative returns ([Jank, Roling & Smajlbegovic](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X20302075)) | Penalise the most shorted; Swedish data more informative |
| Earnings drift | Present only for large firms in one Swedish study ([SSE thesis](http://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=297)) and absent for well-governed firms in another ([Uppsala thesis](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1452207/FULLTEXT01.pdf)); Oslo prices reports within two days ([CBS](https://research.cbs.dk/da/studentProjects/ef598439-563b-45f1-9c6d-3853655cdbc9/)) | Tie-breaker decaying to zero within about 60 trading days |
| Equity issues | One study, whose authors could not be confirmed, finds Oslo private placements' long-run returns indistinguishable from zero ([BI](https://www.bi.no/en/About-bi/employees/department-of-finance/oyvind-norli/)) | Risk flag; dilution feeds per-share data |
| Neglect | Swedish stocks with smaller shareholder bases earn higher returns ([Bodnaruk & Östberg](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2008.01.006)) | Small premium, capped by liquidity |
| Size, seasonality | Nordic size premium negative or ambiguous ([Jyväskylä thesis](https://jyx.jyu.fi/jyx/Record/jyx_123456789_104086)); calendar effects have largely decayed ([seasonality review](https://github.com/luke-cramer/ai-trading/blob/main/research/seasonality-verify.md)) | No weight |

Momentum earns the largest weight because it is the most consistently documented Nordic factor, works in large caps too, and gets cheap crash protection from volatility scaling, which roughly doubled its Sharpe ratio in US data ([Barroso & Santa-Clara](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2014.11.010)). Value and momentum are negatively correlated ([Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12021)), so momentum also serves as a tie-breaker among value names. Value needs a quality filter because profitability controls "dramatically" improve value strategies ([Novy-Marx](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2013.01.003)) and because Nordic small caps lean junky. Factors should be ranked within sectors, because Oslo's mix of energy, shipping, seafood and banks differs sharply from Stockholm's.

Published magnitudes must be haircut. In US data, anomaly returns fall 26% out of sample and **58% after publication** ([McLean & Pontiff](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12365)), 65% of 452 anomalies fail a value-weighted replication ([Hou, Xue & Zhang](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhy131)), and net of spreads in the modern trading era the average anomaly earns about **4 basis points a month** ([Chen & Velikov](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022109022000874)). Outside the US, by contrast, no reliable post-publication decline appears across 39 markets ([Jacobs & Müller](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.06.004)), and factor themes replicate in 93 countries ([Jensen, Kelly & Pedersen](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13249)). A reasonable planning assumption is a forward gross premium of 40–60% of the published Nordic figure. Thesis-level results such as a 29.4% net annual Magic Formula return in the Nordics should be treated as in-sample artefacts ([Poznań review](https://www.journals.ue.poznan.pl/REF/article/view/2790)).

Turnover discipline keeps these premia positive after costs. Anomalies with under 50% monthly turnover generally survive costs when built with a buy/hold spread, meaning stricter rules to enter than to stay ([Novy-Marx & Velikov](https://github.com/danielyang1009/light-speed-engine/blob/6bd63c47eb1a3582377c52033966116c18575953/RFS/Volume%2029/Volume%2029%20-%20Issue%201.md)), whereas momentum's paper profits concentrate in the highest-cost stocks ([Lesmond, Schill & Zhou](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-405X%2803%2900206-X)). With 20 positions of SEK 5,000 on Mini, annual rebalancing at 50% turnover costs about 0.25% a year in courtage, while monthly momentum rebalancing at 30–50% monthly turnover would cost 2–3% a year before spreads.

Commodity and rate data earn their place in the long sleeve as earnings nowcasts rather than trading triggers. Boliden discloses that a 10% move in zinc changes annual operating profit by SEK 950 million and a 10% move in USD/SEK by SEK 2,200 million ([Boliden](https://investors.boliden.com/sites/boliden-ir/files/pr/202502052475-1.pdf)). Salmon farmers' monthly returns are driven mainly by market-wide factors, with salmon prices and harvest volumes secondary ([Misund](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2405851317302283)). DNB repriced customer rates about ten weeks after Norges Bank's May 2026 hike ([DNB call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-dnb-posts-strong-q2-2026-results-as-stock-barely-moves-93CH-4789903)). Valuing commodity producers on mid-cycle rather than spot prices keeps the value sleeve from buying peak earnings in shipping, salmon and metals.

### Small Nordic markets are inefficient, but households are the losers

The user's hypothesis bundles two claims, and the evidence splits them. The first, that small, thinly traded Nordic markets leave anomalies unharvested, is supported. Across 241 anomalies in 39 stock markets, **only the US shows a reliable post-publication decline**, which the authors suggest reflects barriers to arbitrage that segment markets ([Jacobs & Müller](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.06.004)). Momentum works better among stocks with little analyst coverage ([Hong, Lim & Stein](https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00206)). Nordic value pays only when small stocks are included, while momentum works at every size ([Grobys & Huhta-Halkola](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3226899)). Swedish stocks with smaller shareholder bases earn higher returns, consistent with a premium for neglect ([Bodnaruk & Östberg](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2008.01.006)). Strong Nordic shareholder protection should also make insider purchases more informative, since abnormal returns after insider trades rise with country-level protection ([Fidrmuc, Korczak & Korczak](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fagan2888/zhanghaitao1.github.io/HEAD/JBF_7.xml)).

The second claim, that "less advanced traders" are counterparties to exploit, runs the wrong way in the data. Swedish online-broker clients in 1999–2002 **underperformed the market by about 8.5% a year, largely through trading costs**, and concentrated heavy traders did worst ([Anderson](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228265693_Trading_and_Under-Diversification)). Finnish households were contrarians who underperformed momentum-trading foreign investors ([Grinblatt & Keloharju](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-405X%2899%2900044-6)), Norwegian individuals earned negative abnormal returns on stocks from their own industry ([Døskeland & Hvide](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01658.x)), and only high-IQ Finnish investors showed timing and stock-picking skill ([Grinblatt, Keloharju & Linnainmaa](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.05.016)). Professionals do not harvest the inefficiency either: Swedish active equity funds earned −1.47% a year net of fees in 2002–2013, with practically no persistence ([Flam & Vestman](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3082073)). The friction that keeps anomalies alive also eats a small trader's edge. Proportional spreads for small Oslo firms did not fall between 1980 and 2007, and liquidity dried up most for the smallest firms in downturns ([Næs, Skjeltorp & Ødegaard](https://ba-odegaard.no/wps/liquidity_ose/Norges_Bank_Working_Paper_2008_9.pdf)).

The steelman for the user's view deserves a hearing. One Swedish study finds that familiarity-driven holdings were information-driven and profitable ([Massa & Simonov](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhj013)), Finnish individuals' apparent losses around earnings news are largely explained by their use of limit orders ([Linnainmaa](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01576.x)), and FI warns that more than one in five Swedes aged 20–29 trust finfluencer tips, so naive retail flow is real ([FI](https://www.fi.se/sv/publicerat/rapporter/konsumentskyddsrapport/konsumentskyddsrapport-2025-digitalisering-och-virala-trender-skapar-risker-for-konsumenter-pa-finansmarknaden/)). But exploiting naive flow at short horizons requires speed, cheap execution and the ability to provide liquidity, none of which a manual Nordnet user has. Norway's own history is a caution: in the lightly enforced Oslo market of 1985–1992, even insiders earned zero or negative abnormal returns ([Eckbo & Smith](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/danielyang1009/light-speed-engine/HEAD/JF/Volume%2053/Volume%2053%20-%20Issue%202.md)).

The defensible version of the hypothesis is narrower: **an individual can exploit slow information diffusion in neglected, quality-screened small and mid caps by being patient**, holding for six to 24 months in names too small or illiquid for institutions, not by trading faster than other retail investors. The app's short-term sleeve, by contrast, would join exactly the retail flow the evidence says loses money, which is why it should start on paper.

## Fee-aware guardrails turn scores into whole shares

The allocation step converts signal scores into whole-share orders for a user-entered account value. The binding constraints are courtage and evidence, not optimisation theory. The sleeve split should be a fixed, user-editable policy that moves only when the evidence gate below opens.

| Sleeve | Default | Allowed range | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-term value | 85% | 60–100% | Rebalance monthly or quarterly inside no-trade bands |
| Short-term trading | 10% | 0–25%, hard cap | Paper-only until the evidence gate opens; meanwhile the capital waits in cash |
| Cash buffer | 5% | 2–10% | Covers courtage, currency conversion and whole-share residuals |

A performance-chasing split would act on noise. Assuming normally distributed returns, the minimum track record needed to show a positive Sharpe ratio at 95% confidence is about **2,700 trading days (10.8 years) at an annualised Sharpe of 0.5, 680 days (2.7 years) at 1.0 and 270 days (1.1 years) at 1.6** ([Bailey & López de Prado](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1821643)), and comparing two sleeves is harder still. The short sleeve should therefore receive real capital only when three conditions hold on out-of-sample results net of modelled costs: its track length exceeds the minimum track record at the observed Sharpe, skew and kurtosis; its probabilistic Sharpe ratio against zero is at least 0.95; and its deflated Sharpe ratio, computed with the number of variants ever tried, is also at least 0.95 ([Bailey & López de Prado, DSR](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551); [pypbo](https://github.com/esvhd/pypbo)). Even then the Sharpe should be shrunk toward zero by T/(T + T₀), with T₀ of two to three years, and allocations should move at most 5 percentage points a quarter. An alternative is to cap the short sleeve's share of total portfolio volatility at 15–25% and size it with a risk-budgeting estimator ([skfolio](https://pypi.org/project/skfolio/)).

Inside each sleeve, simple weighting beats clever weighting. Across seven datasets, none of 14 optimal-portfolio models consistently beat equal weighting, and sample mean-variance needed more than **3,000 months of data for 25 assets** to do so ([DeMiguel, Garlappi & Uppal](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/22/5/1915/1592901)). The weighting should climb a ladder only as evidence justifies each rung. It starts with equal weights across the top-ranked names, then moves to inverse-volatility weights from 60–120-day volatility, then to equal weights tilted by score but kept within ±50% of equal weight. Black-Litterman views come only after calibration is demonstrated, with expected active returns set to IC × volatility × z-score ([Grinold](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.1989.409211)) and view uncertainty taken from the realised IC's standard error ([PyPortfolioOpt](https://pypi.org/project/pyportfolioopt/)). Hierarchical risk parity is useful as a cluster-cap overlay once a sleeve holds ten or more names. Kelly sizing belongs only as a cap on the short sleeve's gross exposure, at 0.25–0.5 times the estimated Kelly fraction. Half-Kelly keeps about 75% of maximum growth at half the volatility, while betting twice the true Kelly fraction, which is what an overestimated edge produces, yields zero expected growth ([Kelly](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1956.tb03809.x)).

The long-sleeve score should be an equal-weighted average of theme composites (value, quality, volatility-scaled momentum and a low-volatility penalty), each built from within-sector ranks. Small, capped overlays follow for insider net buying, buyback activity, new short positions and retail crowding, with sizes learned from the log. Combining themes with fixed equal weights follows the forecast-combination evidence that simple averages beat estimated weights out of sample ([Smith & Wallis](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0084.2008.00541.x)). The short sleeve works differently. An event creates a candidate; filters remove illiquid, wide-spread, tick-constrained and earnings-window names and any entry against a large adverse overnight move; and a trade is proposed only if the logged expected edge exceeds the round-trip cost from the table above, including currency conversion for cross-border trades.

Hard constraints then shape the weights. The values below are starting heuristics, not empirically fitted thresholds, and the app should label them as such.

| Constraint | Long-term sleeve | Short-term sleeve |
|---|---|---|
| Weight per stock | 15–20% of the sleeve | 25–35% of the sleeve and at most 5% of the account |
| Sector or correlation cluster | 30–35% | 50% |
| Liquidity | Position at most 1–2% of 20-day average daily value | At most 0.5–1% of daily value; quoted spread at most about 0.5% |
| Minimum position | Fee-efficient size, about NOK 19,300 on Norwegian Mini | Same |
| Trading | Skip weight changes under 2–3 points; rebalance monthly or quarterly | At most 2–3 new trades a day, about 10 a week and 3–5 open positions; no averaging down; exit at the forecast horizon |
| Loss limits | Set by policy | Daily 2–3% and weekly 5–6% of the sleeve; a 10–15% drawdown returns the sleeve to paper and triggers a review |

The liquidity caps matter because market impact grows roughly with volatility times the square root of the traded share of volume, so trading 1% of daily volume in a stock with 3% daily volatility costs on the order of 0.3% ([Tóth et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.1694)). Price stop-losses only help when returns show momentum at the relevant horizon, so they should be enabled only if the backtest shows it ([Kaminski & Lo](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2013.07.001)). The fee-efficient minimum position follows from the courtage schedule: the position must be at least the minimum fee divided by the target fee rate, and also clear a liquidity floor. That rule, more than any optimiser, fixes how many names an account can hold.

| Account (NOK) | Long-sleeve positions | Short-sleeve capital at 10% | Short-sleeve positions at ≥NOK 20,000 each |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50,000 | 2–3, or a broad fund core | 5,000 | Paper only |
| 200,000 | 6–8 | 20,000 | 1 |
| 500,000 | 10–15 | 50,000 | 2 |
| 1,000,000 | 15–20 | 100,000 | 3–5 |

A worked example shows how the pieces interact. For a NOK 300,000 account, the default policy puts NOK 15,000 in cash, NOK 255,000 in the long sleeve and NOK 30,000 against the short sleeve, which stays in cash while the sleeve trades on paper. Ten long positions of about NOK 25,500 each sit above the roughly NOK 19,300 level at which Mini's NOK 29 minimum stops binding, so each order costs 0.15%, about NOK 38. A score tilt of −50% would shrink a position to NOK 12,750, below the fee-efficient minimum, so in an account this size the minimum-position rule caps downward tilts at about −24%. The sector cap allows at most three of the ten names in one sector, and whole-share rounding through PyPortfolioOpt's `DiscreteAllocation(weights, latest_prices, total_portfolio_value)` returns the leftover cash to the buffer ([PyPortfolioOpt](https://pypi.org/project/pyportfolioopt/)). A Stockholm name bought from a NOK account would add 0.5% in round-trip currency costs, which a long hold absorbs but a short trade cannot.

The output screen should express uncertainty honestly. Each line should show the score percentile, a calibrated probability of beating the equal-weight universe over the horizon with its historical hit rate and sample size, the expected excess return as a range, the edge net of round-trip cost, and the age and delay of the data behind it. Frequency-framed quantile dotplots help lay users read such probabilities more accurately than continuous densities ([Kay et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/2858036.2858558)). Weights should be shown as whole percentages beside whole-share counts, with each sleeve's "evidence meter" comparing its track length with the minimum track record.

The app should stay personal. Under MiFID II, a recommendation is personal if it is presented as suitable for a person or based on that person's circumstances ([Delegated Regulation 2017/565](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32017R0565); [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2023-07/ESMA35-43-3861_Supervisory_briefing_on_understanding_the_definition_of_advice_under_MiFID_II.pdf)), and FI states that individually tailored advice about stocks requires its permission ([FI](https://www.fi.se/sv/for-konsumenter/spara/till-dig-som-ar-finfluencer/)). An allocation computed from another user's account value is very likely personal advice. Published picks also fall under MAR's investment-recommendation rules even for private persons ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/nyhetsarkiv/nyheter/2022/finanstilsynet-presiserer-at-reglene-for-investeringsanbefalinger-ogsa-gjelder-for-privatpersoner/)). Even for personal use, the collectors should ingest only officially published information as factual triggers and treat forum content as sentiment alone.

## An append-only log turns advice into evidence

Every input, prediction and fill should land in an append-only, bitemporal log that records both when something was true in the world and when the app learned it, so any past recommendation can be reconstructed exactly as it was made ([Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/articles/bitemporal-history.html)). DuckDB with Parquet files is enough at personal scale; version 1.5.6 shipped on 28 September 2026 under the MIT licence ([DuckDB](https://pypi.org/project/duckdb/)).

| Table | One row per | Key fields | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gather_run` | Collector run | Code git SHA, config hash, status, `prev_hash`, `row_hash` | Reproducibility and tamper evidence |
| `source_snapshot` | Fetched payload | Source, URL, HTTP status, `fetched_at`, `published_at`, SHA-256, raw path, parser version, `pit_quality` | Provenance; re-parsing without re-fetching |
| `event` | Parsed item revision | Event key, revision, `supersedes_event_id`, issuer, instrument, type, `published_at`, `first_seen_at`, `available_at`, extractor version, `llm_call_id` | Corrections without overwriting |
| `llm_call` | Extraction call | Model returned, prompt hash, schema version, input hash, request, response, tokens, cost | Replays never resample |
| `price_bar`, `corporate_action`, `fx_rate`, `universe_member` | Instrument-day | Unadjusted OHLCV, dividends, splits, FX, tradability, average daily value, spread, delisting date | Total returns and a survivorship-free universe |
| `model_version`, `feature_value` | Model; feature value | Sleeve, parameters, signal weights, status; `asof_ts`, `data_cutoff_ts`, feature version | Versioned, point-in-time features |
| `prediction` | Instrument × horizon | Score, rank, expected excess return, probability of beating the equal-weight universe, interval, `is_shadow` | What the model believed |
| `recommendation`, `recommendation_line` | Button press; line | Account value, policy, model IDs, data cutoff, reference bid, ask and last with quote delay, estimated courtage, spread and FX, hash of the screen shown | What the user saw |
| `user_action` | Execution | Followed, partial, ignored or override; quantity, price, time, courtage, FX | Separates model skill from behaviour |
| `outcome` | Prediction × horizon | Local and base-currency total return, benchmark and equal-weight-universe returns, excess returns | Scoring |
| `nav_daily` | Book × sleeve × day | NAV, cash, cumulative fees and turnover for paper, shadow and actual books | Implementation shortfall |
| `trial_registry` | Every experiment | Hypothesis, configuration, metric, Sharpe, observation count | Supplies N for the deflated Sharpe ratio |

Four rules make the log trustworthy. A signal's availability time is the later of the source's publication time and the app's first-seen time, and backfilled rows are flagged and given a conservative lag, because a backfill was never knowable at the publication second. Corrections arrive as new rows that supersede old ones, the application role cannot update or delete, and a daily hash-chain head is committed to git. Raw payloads are stored by content hash so new parsers can re-read them. And a point-in-time unit test recomputes random past features and fails the build if any input's availability time falls after the cutoff. Timestamps are stored in UTC, with Oslo and Stockholm trading calendars for horizons.

Outcomes are appended when each horizon matures: the same-day close and 1, 2 and 5 trading days for the short sleeve, and 20, 60, 120 and 250 trading days for the long sleeve. Each is measured in local and base-currency total return against two benchmarks. The first is a total-return index; Euronext's OSEBX factsheet carries the "GI" (gross) label ([Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/sites/default/files/documentation/index-fact-sheets/Oslo_Bors_Benchmark_GI_Index_Factsheet.pdf)), though its dividend treatment should be confirmed, and comparing a dividend-paying portfolio with a price index would overstate alpha by roughly the dividend yield. The second, and the fairer test of stock selection, is an equal-weight index of the app's own tradable universe built with the same filters. The app should keep three books: a paper champion that follows every recommendation at the reference mid-price plus modelled costs, shadow challengers, and the actual account. The gap between the paper and actual books splits, following Perold's implementation-shortfall decomposition, into explicit costs, spread, delay and the opportunity cost of ignored lines ([Perold](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.1988.409150)). When the app runs on delayed quotes, the delay is part of the system's cost and should be logged as such.

Evaluation has to respect how slowly evidence accumulates. Overlapping multi-day returns sampled daily are not independent, so standard errors need non-overlapping samples or HAC corrections.

| Question | Statistic | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Does a signal rank stocks? | Rank information coefficient (IC), its ratio to its own volatility (ICIR), and IC decay across 1–250 days | A weekly ICIR of 0.15 needs about 180 weeks to reach t = 2 and about 400 weeks to reach t = 3 |
| Is a stated probability honest? | Brier score, reliability diagram, isotonic recalibration | Enough predictions in each probability bucket |
| Is a 53% hit rate real? | Binomial test against 50% | About 1,100 independent predictions |
| Should the short sleeve get real money? | Probabilistic and deflated Sharpe ratios and the minimum track record | About 2.7 years at a Sharpe of 1.0; about 10.8 years at 0.5 |
| Is a backtest overfit? | Probability of backtest overfitting via combinatorially symmetric cross-validation | Every variant logged in the trial registry |
| Does a challenger beat the champion? | Paired difference in cost-adjusted excess return | A pre-registered window of at least 26 weeks (short) or 2 years (long) |

The t = 3 bar reflects data-mining across hundreds of published factors ([Harvey, Liu & Zhu](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhv059)), and the overfitting statistic follows Bailey and co-authors ([PBO](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253)). Development should use walk-forward or combinatorial purged cross-validation, which removes training observations whose labels overlap the test period and embargoes those that immediately follow it ([skfolio](https://github.com/skfolio/skfolio/blob/main/src/skfolio/model_selection/_combinatorial.py)). The purge should equal the label horizon: 5 trading days for 5-day labels, 250 for one-year labels. Challengers predict at the same timestamps as the champion and are logged as shadows, so comparisons are paired and market noise cancels. Signal weights should change only on a quarterly schedule, as a blend that moves weight from a prior toward the estimate by T/(T + T₀), with each signal's weight moving at most 5 points per update, negative-IC signals floored at zero, and every change creating a new model version. A new home-grown signal enters only with an out-of-sample t-statistic above 3 and an improvement in purged walk-forward tests.

LLM-derived event features need their own discipline. Sentiment backtests built with LLMs suffer look-ahead bias, because the model may know what happened after the text, and a distraction effect from general knowledge of the company. In that study's in-sample tests, anonymised headlines outperformed named ones, suggesting the distraction effect can outweigh look-ahead bias ([Glasserman & Lin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.17322)). Features computed from documents dated before the model's training cutoff should therefore be flagged as contaminated and their backtest IC treated as an upper bound, with forward logging as the real test. Recent Claude models such as Opus 5.5 reject sampling parameters like `temperature`, and Anthropic notes that temperature 0 never guaranteed identical outputs ([Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/migration-guide.md)). Every extraction should therefore be cached by model, prompt hash, schema version and input hash, and never re-run for an existing key. Structured outputs constrain responses to a JSON schema but cannot be combined with citations, so the client should verify that the quoted evidence span appears in the source text ([Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs.md)). At about 200 announcements a day of roughly 1,500 input and 300 output tokens each, extraction with Haiku 4.5 through the Batch API, which charges half the standard price, costs roughly $0.30 a day, under $10 a month ([Anthropic batch](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/batch-processing.md); [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md)). No public Norwegian or Swedish financial-sentiment dataset was found, and a Swedish BERT sentiment fine-tune reached only 60% accuracy ([Swedish_Sentiment_BERTIL](https://github.com/mosh98/Swedish_Sentiment_BERTIL)), so a hand-labelled set of 300–500 Norwegian and Swedish announcements should gate every model or prompt change.

The tooling is mature. APScheduler 3.11.3 handles scheduling for a single-user app, with Prefect 3.8.7 as the step up when retries and run history matter ([APScheduler](https://pypi.org/project/apscheduler/); [Prefect](https://pypi.org/project/prefect/)). skfolio 1.4.10 covers allocation estimators and purged cross-validation, alphalens-reloaded 0.4.6 gives IC tear sheets, quantstats 0.0.86 book tear sheets, and pypbo the overfitting statistics ([skfolio](https://pypi.org/project/skfolio/); [alphalens-reloaded](https://pypi.org/project/alphalens-reloaded/); [quantstats](https://pypi.org/project/quantstats/); [pypbo](https://github.com/esvhd/pypbo)). MLflow 3.16.1 versions models and prompts, and Evidently 0.7.23 alerts on feature drift ([MLflow](https://pypi.org/project/mlflow/); [Evidently](https://pypi.org/project/evidently/)). Backtrader has had no release since April 2023 ([backtrader](https://pypi.org/project/backtrader/)), and ArcticDB's licence text conflicts with its README on production use ([ArcticDB](https://github.com/man-group/ArcticDB/blob/master/LICENSE.txt)), so both are best avoided.

## Conclusion

The most consequential design choice is not which signal to trust but which data to start hoarding. The registers that carry Nordic short-interest and retail-flow information delete or overwrite their own history, and the formula cannot be judged faster than its track record grows, so a collector that starts in October 2026 builds an asset no vendor sells: a point-in-time, survivorship-free Nordic event history in which every recommendation can be audited. That also reframes the short-term sleeve. For its first years its job is to be an event radar and a paper ledger that sharpens the timing and vetoes of long-sleeve trades, avoiding a buy into a fresh short position or a discounted placement, and only later, if ever, a home for capital. The six required collectors and the Börsdata add-on are enough to begin; the remaining hosts can follow as the log shows which channels earn their keep.

The user's instinct about small markets points at a real inefficiency but the wrong mechanism. Nordic prices are slow where information is scarce and institutions cannot build positions, not where amateurs trade, so an individual's structural advantages are the freedom to own small, illiquid, high-quality names and the patience to wait for diffusion. The data layer is the fragile part. Börsdata moved its API behind a pricier tier in 2025; in 2026 Euronext capped history requests, Shareville changed its name and Nasdaq's old Nordic list pages turned up redirected; next, Norwegian flagging notices may change publisher. The app should expect roughly one breaking source change a year, so the durable advantage lies in the adapters, health checks and the append-only log, not in any single endpoint.
